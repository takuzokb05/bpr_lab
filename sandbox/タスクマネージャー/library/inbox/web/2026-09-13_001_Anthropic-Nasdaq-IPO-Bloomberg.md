## 要約
Anthropic has selected Nasdaq as its stock exchange listing venue for a highly anticipated IPO expected in October 2026. The Claude maker is targeting a valuation that could reach $2 trillion—potentially surpassing SpaceX's $1.77 trillion June 2026 IPO as the largest in history. Anthropic confidentially filed an S-1 draft with the SEC on June 1, 2026. Annual revenue run rate exceeded $65 billion as of July 2026 (up from $9B at end of 2025). The company raised $65 billion in a Series H round at a $965B valuation in May 2026. IPO proceeds will fund continued model development and safety research.
