## 要約
The temporary 50% weekly usage limit boost for Claude Code ends September 13, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT. Starting September 14, users will see a permanent 25% increase over original standard limits—so effective usage headroom drops relative to the promo period but remains higher than pre-May 13 baseline. Practically: Pro plan users had ~50% extra tokens/month during the promo (May 13–Sep 13), now drop to permanent +25%. Max/Team plans follow similar ratios. The article includes a calculator comparing pre-promo, promo, and post-Sep-14 limits for common use cases.
