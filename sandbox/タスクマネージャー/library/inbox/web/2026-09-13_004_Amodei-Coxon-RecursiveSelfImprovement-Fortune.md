The convergence is notable: two voices who were previously antagonistic toward each other's organizations are now agreeing that AI-enabled internet takeover by agent swarms is possible within months. The article contextualizes this within the broader IPO timing, questioning whether Anthropic's public safety warnings and its $2T IPO are in tension.
