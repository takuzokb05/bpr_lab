## 要約
Anthropic confirmed Nasdaq as its IPO listing venue, with investors pricing the offering at approximately $2 trillion—which would make it the largest IPO in history if achieved. The company's annualized revenue exceeded $65B in July 2026, overtaking OpenAI (~$40B). Fable 5.1, Claude Sonnet 5, and Haiku 4.5 are the current revenue drivers. The IPO roadshow is expected to begin in late September 2026, with pricing and listing targeted for October. The move signals AI's transition from VC-backed research to public-market accountability.
