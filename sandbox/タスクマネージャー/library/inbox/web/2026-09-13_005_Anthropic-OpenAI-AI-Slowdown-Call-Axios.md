# Anthropic and OpenAI CEOs Call for Slowdown in AI Development

## 要約
Axios reports that both Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei and OpenAI CEO Sam Altman signed onto a call to slow frontier AI capability development, citing risks of autonomous agent misuse. This is remarkable given the companies' usually competitive relationship. Amodei's proposed 3-step plan includes: (1) industry-wide compute growth pause for safety evaluations, (2) mandatory incident reporting to a cross-governmental body, and (3) kill-switch protocols for deployed agent systems. Market analysts note this could affect GPU demand expectations and AI infrastructure spending in the short term.
