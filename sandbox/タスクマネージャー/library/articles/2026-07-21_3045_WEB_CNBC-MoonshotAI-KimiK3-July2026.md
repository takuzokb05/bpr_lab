## 投稿内容
CNBC report on Kimi K3 launch (July 17, 2026). Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 is a 2.8T-parameter multimodal frontier model with 1M token context, positioned as the first open-weight model to reach frontier scale. Rivals Claude Fable 5 and OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol in overall performance. CNBC coverage positions this as China's serious entry into the global AI frontier race, creating competitive pressure on Anthropic and OpenAI.
