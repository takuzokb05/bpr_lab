## 投稿内容
Al Jazeera primary news coverage (August 6, 2026) explaining what the EU AI Act transparency obligations actually enforced from 2 August 2026. What DID take effect: Article 50 — AI disclosure to users when interacting with chatbots, deepfake labeling requirements, machine-readable markings on AI-generated synthetic media. Enforcement body: EU AI Office + national authorities. Penalties: up to €15M or 3% of global annual turnover. What did NOT take effect: standalone high-risk AI systems (Annex III) — recruitment tools, credit scoring, education, law enforcement, border control, critical infrastructure — deadline pushed to 2 December 2027 (17-month extension). Context: 12 AI companies fined in China's first week of companion-AI rule enforcement (total 4.2M RMB). UK: AI Regulation and Safety Bill cleared the House of Commons.
