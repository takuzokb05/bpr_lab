## 投稿内容
CyberScoop coverage of Senator Mark Warner's AI AGENT Act discussion draft (June 29, 2026). The bill would create an FTC-maintained registry of trusted, secure AI agents, mandate interoperable platform access for authorized agents, and impose strict fiduciary duties on "Custodial User Agents" (CUAs) — autonomous AI systems that manage finances, make commitments, or procure goods on behalf of users. Security focus: verifiable credential requirements for agents, anti-impersonation provisions, and user data protection mandates.
