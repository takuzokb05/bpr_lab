## 投稿内容
DeepSeek official announcement of V4.1-Flash (September 10, 2026). Architecture: 552B-parameter multimodal MoE with new Causal Encoder-Decoder, activates 8B parameters per input token and 16B per output token, 1M context, native image understanding, trained from scratch on 45T tokens. KV cache breakthrough: global KV cache 890 bytes/token (~1/4 of V4-Flash), persistent cache footprint ~1/8. Achieved via FP4 KV cache quantization and cross-layer attention reuse. Pricing: off-peak $0.003/MTok cached input, $0.15/MTok uncached, $0.60/MTok output. MIT open weights. From September 14, 2026 04:00 UTC, all deepseek-v4-pro requests will route to V4.1-Flash at V4.1-Flash rates.
