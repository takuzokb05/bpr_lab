## 投稿内容
MarkTechPost analysis of Fable 5.1/Mythos 5.1 (September 1, 2026, three months after Fable 5). Technical details: 1M token context, 128K max output, adaptive thinking always on. Benchmarks: Terminal-Bench-Science 52.6% (Fable 5: 24.7%, Opus 5: 29.0%), CursorBench 73.4%, SWE-bench Pro 81.2%. Pricing: $10/$50 MTok unchanged, cache reads $0.25 MTok (75% cheaper). Workload cost cuts: general ~25%, highly agentic up to 45%. Same weights as Fable 5, updated safety calibration. Mythos 5.1 same weights with stricter output limits, restricted to vetted US organizations.
