## 投稿内容

The Hacker News (July 2026): NVIDIA and 36 organizations form Open Secure AI Alliance (OSAIA) to develop and share open technologies for securing AI agents.

**NOOA Framework**
- NVIDIA's first contribution: NOOA (No-Operations Open Agents), an Apache 2.0 research framework
- Makes agent behavior easier to test, trace, audit, and govern
- Available on GitHub

**Member Contributions**
- **HPE**: SPIFFE/SPIRE — zero-trust identity that cryptographically verifies AI agents and services
- **Hugging Face**: Safetensors — weights format designed to prevent remote code execution (contributed to PyTorch Foundation)
- **IBM/Red Hat**: Lightwell — distributes digitally signed patches across open-source supply chains
- **Microsoft**: MDASH — multi-model agentic scanning harness that orchestrates AI agents to discover and verify exploitable bugs

**Motivation**
Hugging Face deployed open-weight GLM 5.2 on its own infrastructure to analyze over 17,000 actions during a July 2026 intrusion. Closed AI tools "proved inadequate" by the company's account — this incident was a key motivator for the alliance's formation and the need for open, auditable AI security frameworks.

**Impact**
First major industry coalition specifically targeting AI agent security with open-source tooling. Signals that AI security is moving from proprietary to open-ecosystem model.
