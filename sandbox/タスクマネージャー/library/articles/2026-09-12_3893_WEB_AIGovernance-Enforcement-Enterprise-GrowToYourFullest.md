## 投稿内容
Enterprise perspective on the AI governance enforcement moment (September 2026). EU AI Act: Article 50 (GPAI transparency) now enforced from August 2, 2026; high-risk systems delayed to 2027-2028. US: Colorado ADMT and Chatbot Safety rules advancing; California AI Transparency Act (SB 1000 urgency rewrite) end-of-session; FDA discussion paper on generative AI-enabled medical devices (comments until October 19). Brazil: AI Bill 2338/2023 scheduled for final Senate floor vote September 16, 2026, establishing comprehensive AI legal framework. NIST SP 1353 (AI for Cybersecurity Framework) initial public draft out September 3, open for comment until October 15, 2026. Enterprise practical steps: build AI inventory, risk-assess high-risk uses, document human oversight processes, establish continuous monitoring. Governance is no longer documentation — it is actual enforcement.
