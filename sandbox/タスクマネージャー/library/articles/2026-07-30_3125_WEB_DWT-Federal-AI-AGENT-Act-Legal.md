## 投稿内容
Legal analysis by Davis Wright Tremaine of Senator Mark Warner's AI AGENT Act discussion draft (released June 29, 2026). The Act proposes a federal framework governing how consumer-facing autonomous AI agents interact with large online platforms. Key provisions: (1) mandated interoperable, non-discriminatory platform access for authorized third-party AI agents; (2) FTC registry of trusted, secure AI agents; (3) new legal classification "Custodial User Agent" (CUA) for AI systems managing finances, making commitments, or procuring goods — requiring FTC registration, fiduciary duties, and verifiable credential display. The article critiques the bill as primarily a competition-regulation measure wearing consumer-protection framing, and examines overlap with existing FTC authority and emerging state laws.
