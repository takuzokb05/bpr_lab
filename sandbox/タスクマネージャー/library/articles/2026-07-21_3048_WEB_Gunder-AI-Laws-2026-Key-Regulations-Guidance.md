## 投稿内容
Gunderson Dettmer legal firm's practical guide to 2026 AI laws and regulations. Cross-comparison of US federal, state-level, and EU AI Act developments. Covers Alabama/Georgia healthcare AI restrictions, Colorado/Nebraska mental health AI restrictions, Illinois AI Safety Measures Act. Includes DHS-CISA analysis urging mandatory prompt injection protections and human-override documentation for agentic AI in critical infrastructure. Provides compliance checklist format for AI deployment organizations.
