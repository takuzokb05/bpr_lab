## 投稿内容
BrightDefense summary of EU AI Act enforcement status (August-September 2026). From August 2, 2026: AI Office and EU Member State authorities responsible for implementing, supervising, and enforcing the AI Act. Currently enforcing: Article 50 (transparency for general-purpose AI) and GPAI system requirements. High-risk system requirements delayed to 2027-2028. September 2026 inspection focus: automated resume screening (HR), algorithmic credit assessment (retail banking), AI triage tools (private healthcare). AI Office enforcement powers: requests for information, model evaluations, on-site inspections, staff interviews. Penalties: up to €35M or 7% worldwide annual turnover for prohibited AI practices; €15M or 3% for Article 50 violations. December 2, 2026: Article 50(2) transition period ends for covered generative AI systems placed on market before Aug 2, 2026.
