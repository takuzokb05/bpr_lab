## 投稿内容
Google released three new Gemini models on July 21, 2026. Gemini 3.6 Flash is priced at $1.50/$7.50 per million tokens with 17% fewer output tokens than 3.5 Flash, DeepSWE coding benchmark up from 37% to 49%, knowledge cutoff advanced from January 2025 to March 2026. OSWorld-Verified computer use improved from 78.4% to 83.0%. Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite ($0.30/$2.50 per M tokens) is the fastest in the series at 350 output tokens/second, achieving 54% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and 74.0% on OSWorld-Verified. Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber is a restricted security-focused model for governments and trusted partners via the CodeMender pilot program. Google confirmed it has begun pretraining for Gemini 4. Gemini 3.5 Pro remains unshipped after multiple missed deadlines.
