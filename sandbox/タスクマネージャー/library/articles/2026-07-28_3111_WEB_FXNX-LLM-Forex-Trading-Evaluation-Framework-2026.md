## 投稿内容
FXNX published a 2026 framework for evaluating LLMs for forex trading with real data. Evaluation axes: 1) Signal accuracy (backtest vs live divergence rate), 2) Cost efficiency (API cost÷win rate), 3) Latency (can it maintain time-advantage in forex), 4) Risk explanation ability (pre-warning of drawdowns). Conclusion: LLM standalone signal generation fails to beat simple baselines in 2-year backtests. Most LLM trading strategies lose money after fees. However, LLMs genuinely help in: sentiment analysis on news/earnings, code generation (EA/strategy code), risk documentation. Most effective architecture is hybrid: ensemble of price data + order book + LLM signals. Claude+MCP via MT5 records lowest latency in their testing. AI trading market: $27.85B in 2026, growing to $45.74B by 2030.
