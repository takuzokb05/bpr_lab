# EU AI Act Enforcement Starts August 2, 2026: EC Official Press Release

## 投稿内容
From 2 August 2026, AI Office and national authorities begin enforcing the AI Act. Transparency rules: chatbots must disclose AI interaction; deepfakes must be labelled; AI-generated content requires machine-readable marks. Penalties: up to €15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover. AI Omnibus delays high-risk AI system rules to December 2, 2027 and regulated-product high-risk AI to August 2, 2028. AI systems generating non-consensual sexual content banned from December 2, 2026.
