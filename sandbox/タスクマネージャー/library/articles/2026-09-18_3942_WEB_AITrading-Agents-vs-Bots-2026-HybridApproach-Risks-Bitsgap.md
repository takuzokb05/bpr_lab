## 投稿内容
AI trading agents vs traditional bots: a nuanced comparison for 2026. Bots execute predefined rules deterministically; agents use LLMs to interpret information, form plans, and revise strategy mid-run. Agents are more flexible but less predictable and harder to backtest rigorously. Documented TradingAgents result: ~7% returns over 30 days vs S&P500's 4.5%, but with 22% drawdowns and no repeatability guarantee. The gap between simulated and live returns remains large due to transaction costs, slippage, and market regime changes. Recommended 2026 approach: AI for research and filtering, transparent rule-based automation for execution under defined risk parameters.
