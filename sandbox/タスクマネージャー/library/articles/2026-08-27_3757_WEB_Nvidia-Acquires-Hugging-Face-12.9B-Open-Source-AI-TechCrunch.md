## 投稿内容
Nvidia has agreed to acquire Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, according to The Information. Business Insider reported the talks haven't produced a signed agreement yet and could still fall through. Hugging Face is one of the most popular hubs where developers share and download open source AI models.

Deal terms: $12.9 billion, up dramatically from the $4.5 billion valuation Hugging Face received at its last funding round in 2023. Hugging Face previously rejected a $500 million investment from Nvidia in 2025 (then valued at $7B). Annual revenue approximately $150 million, approaching profitability. CEO Clem Delangue has publicly supported Nvidia's open-source initiatives throughout 2026.

Strategic rationale for Nvidia: strengthens position in open-source AI, provides leverage to keep customers on Nvidia hardware, enables re-entry into cloud computing, creates outlet for unused computing capacity from customer commitments.
