## 投稿内容

This AI trading idea made me look like a fool.

I've been calibrating the ADX indicator for over 20 years. Thousands of parameter combinations across every major index market. So when I asked AI to improve my ADX filter on an E-mini NASDAQ strategy, I expected something marginal.

It proposed replacing part of my logic entirely.

Original strategy: $87K net profit, $23K drawdown.
After AI's filter: $200K+ net profit, $13K drawdown.

Drawdown cut nearly in half. Profit more than doubled.

The strangest part? The AI's suggestion was simpler than what I had. Not more complex. Simpler. It took one piece of the ADX logic and replaced it with a cleaner, more direct measurement of what ADX was trying to capture in the first place.

This doesn't mean AI replaces experience. I still had to evaluate whether the improvement was genuine or just overfitting. I still ran it across multiple markets and out-of-sample data.

But AI spotted a pattern in indicator behavior that 25 years of screen time missed. When that happens, you'd be foolish to ignore it.

What's your experience using AI to improve strategies?
