## 投稿内容
Anthropic has debuted Claude Opus 5, the company's latest and most powerful everyday model, with a standout feature that lets users toggle between three effort levels (low, medium, high) to balance cost and capability. Released July 24, 2026, Opus 5 is priced at $5/$25 per million input/output tokens — identical to Opus 4.8 — while delivering near-Fable 5 performance. On CursorBench 3.2 it scores within 0.5% of Fable 5 at half the cost; on OSWorld 2.0 it surpasses Fable 5's best result at one-third the price. Designed for complex, long-running agentic coding workflows, the model verifies its own work and recovers from errors without human intervention.
