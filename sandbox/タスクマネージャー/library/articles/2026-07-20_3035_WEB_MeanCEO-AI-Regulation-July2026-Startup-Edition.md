AI law now affects how you build, sell, document, and review your product, not just what your lawyer does later. Key July 2026 developments: (1) UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance in Geneva (July 6-7); (2) EU Commission published transparency guidelines (July 20); (3) FTC seeking public comment on AI accuracy policy statement (comments due July 31); (4) Multiple bipartisan AI bills in Congress (chatbots, digital replicas, nonconsensual AI imagery, AI standards); (5) At least 35 AI-related state laws enacted — Alabama & Georgia prohibit health insurer AI adverse decisions without human review; New York passed 5 AI laws (kids chatbot safety, training data transparency, FAIR News Act, data center moratorium, AI surveillance pricing ban).
