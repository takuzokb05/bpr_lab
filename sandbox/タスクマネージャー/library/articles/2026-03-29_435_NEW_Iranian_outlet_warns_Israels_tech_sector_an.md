🗞️ NEW: Iranian outlet warns Israel’s tech sector and AI data centers “now a target” after strikes on Iran’s steel industry

Iran’s Fars News says the U.S.-backed Israeli attack on what it called “the backbone of Iran’s steel industry” has “changed the rules of the game,” warning that Israel’s most profitable sectors—including AI data centers—are now in scope for retaliation.

➤ The report says Israel’s tech sector accounts for ~20% of GDP and over 50% of exports, describing it as “the backbone of the regime’s economy”

➤ It adds that Iran “can also target the most profitable industries… that provide a major part of its exports and foreign currency revenues”

➤ Companies listed include Check Point Software Technologies, CyberArk Software, monday, Wix, Tower Semiconductor, Nova, Camtek, Mobileye Global, Nvidia, Nebus Group, Intel fabrication plants (fabs 8, 28, and 38), and Safe Super Intelligence (SSI)

The claims reflect messaging from Iranian IRGC-linked media rather than confirmed targeting decisions from the military body.
