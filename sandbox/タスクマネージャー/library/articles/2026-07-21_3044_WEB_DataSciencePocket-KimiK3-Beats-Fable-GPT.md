## 投稿内容
Moonshot AI released Kimi K3 on July 16, 2026. Specs: 2.8 trillion parameters, 1M token context window, native vision capability, first open-weight model at 2.8T scale (full weights scheduled July 27, API-only until then). Benchmarks: FrontierSWE score 77.8 (1st globally), beating GPT-5.6 Sol (77.6) and Fable 5 (76.8). Artificial Analysis leaderboard rank: #4 globally. Pricing roughly 1/3 of GPT-5.6 Sol. Signals end of "cheap Chinese AI" era while raising the bar.
